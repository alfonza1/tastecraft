## Community rules checked November 17, 2026

- **r/LLMDevs:** the exported agent skill is a relevant workflow discussion. Its [moderator announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/LLMDevs/comments/1mvuw5x/community_rule_update_clarifying_our/) permits free open-source projects without advance approval, prohibits commercial promotion, and forbids disguised advertising. Disclose authorship and show an actual exported profile; check current rules before posting.
- **r/SideProject:** a possible audience for the working studio and a specific feedback question. Its [rules endpoint](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/about/rules/) did not expose rule text to this research client. Read sidebar/submission instructions before sharing; an empty response does not establish unrestricted promotion permission.
- **r/opensource:** excluded. [Recent reporting](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/reddits-r-opensource-bans-ai-made-and-ai-focused-projects-also-bans-non-english-speakers-sweeping-new-rules-spark-backlash-over-linux-kernel-contributions-and-solo-developers) describes restrictions on AI-assisted and new solo projects. The official endpoint did not expose the complete policy for independent verification.

The [Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) require a usable project, discourage quickly generated one-offs, and prohibit vote solicitation. Link the live studio. The maintainer should first apply a profile to a real project and be available to discuss the result and implementation. No virality or user adoption is claimed.
